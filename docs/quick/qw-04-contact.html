Quick win 4 of 21 · Getting people to the right place

Make Contact us ask what you need

One question, one answer, instead of eight panels of email addresses.

Now

Contact us is eight accordion panels of email addresses, grouped by team rather than by what the visitor wants. Two smaller things: the switching address has a full stop inside its link, and the London map pin is labelled Ibex House while the address above it reads Riverscape.

With this change

Contact the Service Centre

For incidents, service requests and anything affecting live service. You can also raise and track incidents in the Self-Service Interface.

servicecentre@smartdcc.co.uk

Contact our High Level Escalations team

If you're unhappy with how an issue has been handled, or with any other part of our service, we'll investigate and keep you updated.

complaints@smartdcc.co.uk

Contact Customer Engagement

For questions about charges and finance updates. For a forum or event, contact its owner directly.

customerengagement@smartdcc.co.uk

Contact our finance team

For invoice queries and file submissions. From 2 November 2026 invoices come from DCC 2 Ltd.

finance@smartdcc.co.uk customerinvoices@smartdcc.co.uk

Contact the switching team

For the development and running of the Central Switching Service.

contact.switching@smartdcc.co.uk

Contact our communications team

media@smartdcc.co.uk

Contact Procurement

procurement@smartdcc.co.uk

See our careers site

All open roles are on our careers site. For anything else about recruitment:

DCC.Recruitment@smartdcc.co.uk
