Five ideas for smartdcc.co.uk

Clickable prototypes of five improvements to the Smart DCC website, timed around the move to DCC2 in November. Each one is shown in DCC's own colours, type and photography, so you can see how it would sit on the live site.

Version 2 28 September 2026 Designed prototypes Live figures and sample data

The prototypes

  1. The front door
    1

    The front door

    A homepage that asks who you are and shows what's live this week, headline numbers everyone can read, a page explaining the new ownership, and a proper route for members of the public.

    Try: pick "I have a smart meter" in the homepage panel, then choose a problem on the help page.

    Open prototype
  2. Consultations
    2

    Consultations and engagement

    Open consultations first with days left, an online response form that saves as you go, a strip showing each consultation's progress to DCC's conclusions, email alerts and a forums calendar.

    Try: open the VWAN consultation, start a response, leave the page and come back to it.

    Open prototype
  3. Find anything
    3

    Find anything

    Search that understands plain questions and acronyms, a Document Centre with filters that combine and versions that say which is current, and a searchable glossary.

    Try: search "my smart meter is not working", then filter the Document Centre to Charging in RY 2026/27.

    Open prototype
  4. Status and performance
    4

    Network status and performance

    A public status page with incidents and planned maintenance, the performance charts rebuilt from DCC's own figures with tables and downloads, and a programme tracker with real stages and dates.

    Try: on Network performance, tab through a chart's months, then download the CSV.

    Open prototype
  5. Joining the network
    5

    Joining the network

    Choose your type of organisation and see your whole route on one page, with timescales, definitions where terms appear, the digital onboarding tool where it applies and an enquiry form that reaches the right team.

    Try: choose "Energy services or data company", tick off two steps, then press Get in touch.

    Open prototype

How to review

  • Everything interactive works: filters, search, the response form and its saved draft, charts, downloads, the onboarding route and every form. Links to pages outside these five go nowhere by design.
  • Use the bar at the top to move between prototypes, and the grey bar below it to move between pages within one. Switch Notes on (top right) to see what each page proposes and why, plus notes on behaviour (yellow) and open questions for DCC (red). Notes start switched off.
  • Figures marked live, such as meter numbers and the performance series, come from smartdcc.co.uk on 28 September 2026. Service states, incidents, timescales and dates for events are sample data.
  • Photography is DCC's own, loaded from smartdcc.co.uk. Where it can't load, a brand-colour illustration shows instead. DCC's own header and footer aren't reproduced. Where a change to the header is part of the idea, it's drawn as a simple block.
  • Works on a phone: open this page on your mobile to check the layouts.
