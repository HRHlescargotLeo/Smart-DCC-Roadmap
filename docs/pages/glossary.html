Prototype 3 of 5 · Glossary

What we're proposing

The glossary as a web page, searchable and linked from the header, with each term addressable so any page can link to it. Where a term appears in running text, it can show its definition in place, without leaving the page.

Why

The six onboarding steps on Partner with DCC use DUIS, SMKI, SREPT, RAPP and UEPT, and spell out some of them. The DCC glossary exists, but as a 145 KB PDF linked from What we're working on. A new entrant reading the onboarding steps would need both open side by side.

Definitions are our own drafts for this prototype and need checking by DCC. Try it in a sentence: new entrants need a and a interface.

Glossary

Plain definitions of the terms and acronyms used across this site.

BehaviourTyping filters the list and greys out letters with no terms. Every term has its own address, such as glossary.html#term-duis, so a search result or a definition pop-up can link straight to it.
