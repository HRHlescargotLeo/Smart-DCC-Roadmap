Prototype 1 of 4 · Help for the public

What we're proposing

Keep the existing consumer page, which is honest and clear, and make it findable: a link in the header, and a single question at the top that gives one answer rather than ten. Add the contact form the page already refers to.

Why

Today the page is reached through Contact us, then the "Members of the public" panel. Searching the site for "my smart meter is not working" returns nothing, even though an FAQ has almost exactly that title. Two of the answers ask people to "complete the contact us form", and the Contact us page has no form on it.

Answer text is adapted from the live consumer page. The contact form is new.

Help with your smart meter

We run the secure network that carries messages between smart meters and energy suppliers. We can't see or change your meter, your bill or your display, but we can point you to the people who can.
