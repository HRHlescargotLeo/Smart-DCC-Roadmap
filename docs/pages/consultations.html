Prototype 2 of 5 · Consultations and engagement

What we're proposing

A consultations listing that puts open consultations first and says how long is left, filters by topic and audience, and lets people sign up for alerts. Each consultation gets an online response form and a strip showing where it is in its life, from open to DCC's conclusions. Forums and events get a calendar.

Why

The listing already has status filters, which is more than many sites manage. But open and closed consultations look the same, dates are given as a range to work out for yourself, and a consultation and DCC's conclusions on it appear as two unrelated listings. Responding means downloading a Word template and emailing it, and on the VWAN consultation the email link doesn't work: the full stop after the address is inside the link. We found no way to be told when a new consultation opens.

Titles, statuses and dates are the live listing on 28 September 2026. Topics and audiences are our tagging. The upcoming budget consultation is a sample.
