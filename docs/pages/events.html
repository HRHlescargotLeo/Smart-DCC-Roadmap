Prototype 2 of 4 · Forums and events

What we're proposing

A calendar of the forums, webinars and test events customers can attend, with who each one is for, a register button and an "add to calendar" file.

Why

Customer Engagement describes six ways to engage, from the Operations Forum to the Design Release Forum, without a single date. News & Events offers a filter for past events and none for upcoming ones. The forums clearly happen; the website just doesn't say when.

Forum names are live. Dates, the briefing and the transition webinar are sample.

Forums and events

Regular forums for our customers, plus briefings and test events. Most are online.
