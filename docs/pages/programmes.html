What we're proposing

One card per programme with its stage, status and next milestone, linked to its documents and consultations. Below it, a scorecard against the four strategic outcomes in the Business and Development Plan, ready for the strategy and technology roadmap the new licence asks DCC2 to publish.

Why

The page promises "estimated delivery dates" and then groups programmes by year as a plain list, so the only thing a reader learns about MHHS is that it finishes in 2026. The documents panel links the 2024/25 Business and Development Plan. SECCo's 2026 survey, of much the same audience, found people wanted more visibility of progress against strategy, and DCC2 will have to publish a roadmap anyway.

Programme names and target years are live. Stages, milestones, progress and scorecard measures are sample.
