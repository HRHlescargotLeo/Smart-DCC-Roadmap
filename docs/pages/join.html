Joining the networkPartner enquiry

Prototype 4 of 4 · Joining the network

What we're proposing

Start by asking what kind of organisation you are, then show that route in full: every step on one page, who you deal with, typical timescales, the terms explained where they appear, and the digital onboarding tool for the organisations it serves. Innovators who only want to test a product are told so early, rather than working through six steps they don't need.

Why

Partner with DCC shows six onboarding steps in a carousel, one at a time, the same for every kind of organisation, with four of them linking to "here". DCC has since launched a digital onboarding tool for Other Users, the largest group in the pipeline according to its own announcement, and the partner page doesn't mention it. SECCo's survey also asked for better onboarding support, which suggests the problem isn't only on DCC's side.

The six steps are live. Timescales are sample figures for DCC to replace. Customer quotes are left as placeholders pointing at the existing testimonials.
