Prototype 1 of 4 · The front door

What we're proposing

A homepage that asks who you are and sends you somewhere useful, shows what's live this week, and gives the headline numbers in a form everyone can read. Around it, a notice about the move to DCC2 that links to a new page explaining it, and a route for members of the public that doesn't start at Contact us.

Why

The current homepage runs hero, meter counter, video, innovation and careers, and none of it changes with the people arriving. At least three consultations are open as of today, one closing on 2 October, and the homepage mentions none of them. The business moves to DCC2 in November, which the site only mentions in News. And the public, who arrive in numbers we don't know but can guess at, find their page only through the "Members of the public" panel on Contact us.

Headline figures are live from smartdcc.co.uk on 28 September 2026. Links in the audience panels go to pages in this pack where one exists.
