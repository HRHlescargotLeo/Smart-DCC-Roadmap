Governance & Regulations

The Smart Meter Communication Licence

From 2 November 2026, DCC's services are delivered by DCC 2 Ltd, a wholly owned subsidiary of SECCo Ltd, under the second Smart Meter Communication Licence. Ofgem awarded the licence in April 2026 after a review of the DCC regulatory framework and a competitive tender.

The licence sets a not-for-profit operating model and an ex-ante price control, under which our costs are agreed with Ofgem in advance. It is regulated by Ofgem and can be modified by the Department for Energy Security and Net Zero or Ofgem.
