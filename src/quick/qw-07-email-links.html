Fix the broken email links

Two links send email to addresses that don't exist, because of a stray full stop.

Now

  • VWAN consultation, "Please email your response…"mailto:consultations@smartdcc.co.uk.
  • Contact us, Switching queriesmailto:contact.switching@smartdcc.co.uk.

In both, the full stop that ends the sentence is part of the link, so the email goes to an address ending ".uk." and bounces.

With this change

PageLink after the fix
VWAN consultationconsultations@smartdcc.co.uk
Contact us: Switching queriescontact.switching@smartdcc.co.uk

Shown as text with a copy button, so the address works whether or not the visitor has an email app set up.
