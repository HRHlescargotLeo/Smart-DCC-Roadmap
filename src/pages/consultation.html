What we're proposing

Each consultation page shows its status and deadline at the top, where it sits in its life from open to DCC's conclusions, and the documents in one list. Respondents can answer online, question by question, save a draft and come back, and get a receipt. The Word template stays for anyone who prefers it.

Why

On the live VWAN consultation the only way to respond is to download a Word form and email it to an address whose link doesn't work, because the full stop after it is part of the link. Online responses are also easier for DCC to analyse, which matters when a consultation draws responses from dozens of parties. Whether DCC wants that is a fair question: some respondents will want to attach a formal letter whatever form we give them.

Opens on the VWAN consultation. Try another from the listing: a closed one, a DCC response, or the sample upcoming one, to see how the page changes.
