What we're proposing

A public status page: each service with its current state, open incidents with timed updates, resolved incidents for the last 90 days, and planned maintenance that can be filtered by service and added to a calendar. The monthly Category 1 PDFs stay as the formal record.

Why

Today, incidents are published after the event as one PDF per month (August 2026: no Category 1 incidents, which is good news delivered in a 158 KB file). Planned maintenance is a 528 KB annual PDF on the Customer Engagement page. Customers already get live incident information through the Self-Service Interface, so the question is less whether this information exists than whether a public version helps. We think it does, for smaller customers and for anyone trying to answer "is it us or the network?"

Service names are real. Their states, the incidents and the maintenance windows are sample data.
