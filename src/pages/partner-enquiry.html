What we're proposing

The partner enquiry form asks what kind of organisation you are and what you're interested in, shows which team it will go to, and asks the one or two extra questions that team needs. Other Users are pointed to the digital onboarding tool before they fill anything in.

Why

The live form asks for name, job title, company, email and "How can we work together?", then a consent tick. Everything that tells DCC who should answer it arrives as free text, so someone has to read each one and pass it on. Two questions at the top do that job instead.

Team names and response times are sample, for DCC to confirm.
