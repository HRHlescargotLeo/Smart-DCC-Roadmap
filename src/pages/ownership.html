What we're proposing

One page that explains the change of licence holder: who DCC2 is, when the transfer happens, what changes for customers and what doesn't, with the questions customers are likely to ask. The licence and regulation page and Who we are would link here rather than each telling part of the story.

Why

The licence page still says the licence came into effect in September 2013 and "is 12 years long", which by now reads as though DCC is operating without one. Everything about DCC2 lives in News, where it's split across four articles in date order. Customers will want the answer in one place before November, and so, presumably, will Ofgem.

Dates and facts are from Ofgem's licensee decision and DCC's own news. All wording is a draft for DCC and DCC2 to check.

About DCC

Our new ownership

From November 2026, DCC is owned by DCC2 Ltd, a not-for-profit subsidiary of the Smart Energy Code Company (SECCo). The network, our services and the people you deal with stay the same.

Last updated 28 September 2026

How we got here

  1. 23 Sep 2013The first Smart Meter Communication Licence starts, held by Smart DCC Ltd, part of Capita, for 12 years.
  2. 9 Feb 2026Ofgem selects DCC2 Ltd as the next licence holder after a competitive tender.
  3. 2026Ofgem formally awards the new licence to DCC2. DCC and DCC2 start joint handover planning.
  4. NowHandover in progress. Services continue as normal.
  5. November 2026The business transfers from Capita to DCC2.

Open questionWe have the selection date from Ofgem but not the formal award date or the exact transfer date. DCC to confirm both, and whether the transfer date can be published now.

What changes and what stays the same

What changes

  • DCC becomes a not-for-profit organisation, run to deliver value for consumers.
  • A majority of the board will be independent.
  • Our costs are agreed with Ofgem in advance, rather than reviewed afterwards.
  • We'll publish a business strategy and a technology roadmap.

What stays the same

  • The smart metering network and the services we provide.
  • Your contacts, including the Service Centre.
  • The Smart Energy Code and how you raise incidents and requests.
  • This website and its address.
